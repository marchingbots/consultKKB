Land Advisory

Land acquisition in Indonesia requires careful due diligence and an understanding of zoning regulations, ownership structures, and regional government rules.

We assist clients in evaluating land opportunities, performing legal verification, and structuring compliant ownership models for foreign investors.

Licensing

Indonesia’s licensing framework involves multiple government systems including OSS and sector-specific approvals.

We guide clients through the full licensing process ensuring their operations remain compliant while minimizing delays.

PT PMA Structuring

A PT PMA allows foreign investors to legally operate businesses in Indonesia while complying with investment regulations.

We assist clients in determining the correct business classification (KBLI), ownership structure, and investment requirements for sustainable operations.

Pariwisata Strategy

Indonesia’s tourism sector offers significant opportunity for international investors.

We help clients navigate the legal, operational, and regulatory framework required to operate tourism businesses such as villas, resorts, and hospitality ventures.
